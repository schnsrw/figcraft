What's next.

The current focus is the distributed relay, so more than one relay can serve rooms safely.

Storage work comes first: the relay load test found that commits and cold restores ran one at a time on the relay's hub thread, so a large restore could stall other rooms for seconds. That work moves off the hub thread before multi-replica work begins.

  • Room leases in PostgreSQL beside the existing fencing tokens.
  • Routing to the room owner: any relay redirects a client to the current owner.
  • Graceful drain that snapshots rooms and signals clients to reconnect during rolling releases.
  • Viewer and commenter enforcement at relay message level, plus room, connection and rate quotas.
  • Two-replica and kill tests: stale owners fenced, no confirmed edit lost before or after a crash.
